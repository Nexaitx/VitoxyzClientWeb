OUR VERIFICATION STANDARD

How we verify our staff

Every applicant submits their nursing or paramedical qualification documents, which we check against the issuing institution. They then sit an interview covering practical scenarios, not just paperwork. Finally, we run background verification including identity and address checks.

After each placement, the family rates the staff member. Those ratings stay on the profile, and anyone who falls below our standard is removed from the panel.
