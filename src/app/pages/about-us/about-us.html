About Vitoxyz

A health platform built in Kharar, for families across Mohali and the Tricity.

OUR STORY

We started because of a 10pm chemist run

Anyone who has cared for a sick family member in Mohali knows the routine. The fever spikes at night. The strip of tablets in the drawer is empty. You drive to the nearest chemist, who is shut, then the next one, who has the wrong brand, then a third who has it but not in the strength the doctor wrote down.

Vitoxyz began in a small office at 413, Sunny Business Centre in Sunny Enclave, Kharar, with one aim — to take that run off your evening. We started by delivering medicines. Families kept asking us for something else: someone qualified to come home and change a dressing, give an injection, or sit with an elderly parent. So we built that too.

Today Vitoxyz does three things, and does them properly. We deliver genuine medicines and health products. We place verified nurses and care staff in homes. And we build diet plans with qualified dietitians. That is the whole list. We would rather be trusted for three services than average at ten.

OUR SERVICES

What we do

01

Medicines and health products

Everything from prescription medicines and daily supplements to skin care, baby care, elder care and personal hygiene. Stock is sourced from licensed distributors, and every invoice carries the batch number and expiry date so you can check exactly what arrived at your door.

02

Verified home care staff

Nurses, patient attendants, elderly care staff, physiotherapists and newborn care attendants. Every person on our panel is qualification-checked, interviewed and background-verified before we send them to a family. You see their profile before you confirm.

03

Diet plans

Not a generic PDF. You share your health history, your routine and the food you actually eat. A qualified dietitian builds a plan around that, then adjusts it as your results come in.

OUR PROMISE

How we work

01

Genuine, or we do not stock it

Every product we sell comes through licensed distributors with a verifiable supply chain. Batch and expiry go on the invoice. If a product cannot be traced properly, it does not go on our shelves.

02

Prescriptions are checked by a pharmacist

Prescription medicines are dispensed only against a valid prescription from a registered doctor. A registered pharmacist reviews every one before the order is packed. This slows some orders down by an hour. We think that is the right trade.

03

Staff are verified before they reach your door

Qualification documents, an interview, and background verification. After every placement, the family rates the staff member, and those ratings stay visible.

04

Your data stays yours

Prescriptions and health details are used to process your order and nothing else. We do not sell customer data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

A CLEAR LINE

What we do not do

It is worth being clear about this, because health platforms often blur the line.

Vitoxyz does not provide online doctor consultation. We do not diagnose conditions, we do not prescribe medicines, and nothing on this website is a substitute for advice from a registered medical practitioner. If you are unwell, please see a doctor. Once you have a prescription, we will get it filled and delivered.

Our dietitians work on nutrition and lifestyle. They do not replace your treating doctor, and they will not ask you to stop any medication.

Why we are clear about this

Publishing a clear statement of what we do not do is one of the strongest trust signals a health business can give. It also helps make sure customers understand exactly where Vitoxyz fits into their healthcare journey.
