Common questions

How fast is medicine delivery in Mohali?

Most orders placed within our Mohali and Kharar service area reach you the same day. Urgent orders are usually delivered within a few hours, depending on stock and your location.

Do I need a prescription to order?

Over-the-counter items, supplements and personal care products don't need one. For prescription medicines, upload a valid prescription from a registered doctor and our pharmacist will verify it before dispatch.

Are the nurses you send actually qualified?

Yes. Every nurse and attendant goes through qualification checks, an interview, and background verification before joining our panel. You'll see their profile and rating before you confirm a booking.

What areas do you serve?

Kharar, Sunny Enclave, Sector 70, Phase 3B2, Aerocity, Landran, Mullanpur, Zirakpur, Chandigarh and Panchkula. Not sure about your pincode? Call +91 7355400002 and we'll tell you straight away.

How do diet plans work?

You share your health history, routine and food preferences. A qualified dietitian builds a plan around them, then follows up to adjust it as your results come in.
